GPA conversion notes — Faezeh Absalan

These are unofficial, credit-weighted estimates using McGill University's
published conversion scale for Iran, rather than a linear scaling of the
overall average. They are not a WES evaluation or a receiving university's
official determination.

Source:
https://www.mcgill.ca/gradapplicants/how-apply/you-apply-mcgill/equivalency

Formula: sum(course credits * converted grade points) / sum(graded credits).
Incomplete thesis registrations and semester withdrawals are not counted as
additional graded courses. The completed seven-credit thesis is counted once.
